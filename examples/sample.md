# Why I stopped checking email first

## The problem

For years I treated email as the start of my workday. It felt responsible. Someone might need me, so I should look right away. I also told myself that a clear inbox meant a clear head, even though the inbox never stayed clear for long.

I used to start every morning by opening my email. Within ten minutes I was answering other people's questions and my own plans were gone. By noon I felt busy but had nothing to show for it.

## What I do now

Now I write for the first hour. The phone stays in another room and the inbox stays closed. I pick one thing that matters and work on it until it is done or the hour is up. Some days that is a report. Other days it is a hard decision I have been avoiding.

Nothing bad has happened. People who need me urgently call, and everyone else waits a few hours. Most replies are better after a clear head and a finished task. I also send fewer messages now, because I answer fewer questions that were never really mine.

If you try it, start small. Give yourself thirty minutes for a week. Notice how the rest of the day feels, then decide if it is worth keeping. You can always go back to the old way. I never have.
